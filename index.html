    Before you ask

    Fair questions.

    Why isn't my pass instant?

    Because a human checks every payment against our account. It's how we skip a payment gateway and its fees. Expect a few hours, faster during the day.

    Where do I find the reference number?

    GPay: tap the payment → UPI transaction ID, 12 digits. PhonePe: transaction details → UTR. Paytm: UPI Ref No. Any of them works.

    I paid the wrong amount.

    Message the organisers before submitting. Underpaid, we'll ask for the difference. Overpaid, we'll return it. Don't pay twice.

    Can I pay cash instead?

    Ask the organisers. We'd rather have it online so the headcount for food and seating is accurate, but we can add you manually and issue a code.

    Can I bring a friend from another programme?

    This one is BMS-01 and BMS-02 only. Passes are per person with your name on them.

    Is a refund possible?

    Up to the registration deadline, yes — message the organisers. After that we've already committed the spend on food and setup.
